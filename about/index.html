About JobPath India

A source-first guide to Indian government recruitment and competitive examinations.

JobPath India is an independent information project for students, graduates, working professionals and people preparing for public recruitment examinations. Our aim is to make official hiring notices easier to discover and understand, especially when rules span many recruiting organizations, regions and qualification categories.

We organize important details such as the recruiting authority, advertisement number, eligibility criteria, deadlines and original application links. We also publish educational guidance on how to read an official notification, prepare supporting documents, distinguish an exam calendar from a vacancy, and recognize recruitment scams.

Information is not a substitute for the official notice. Recruitment rules, vacancies, examination schedules and fees can change after a summary is published. Each reader should open the issuing authority's current notice, review any corrigenda and complete applications through the authorized portal.

We do not represent the Government of India, any state government, a public service commission or a recruitment board. We do not collect application fees or promise examination success. Our editorial priorities are practical instructions, transparent sourcing, labeling of illustrative content, responsible correction handling and accessible mobile navigation.

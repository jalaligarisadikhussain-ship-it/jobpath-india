THE RULES

Giveaway Terms

IMPORTANT: Preview only. This website is an interactive demonstration and no real prize is currently offered. An organizer must publish their legal identity, contact address, verified prize inventory, eligibility criteria, closing deadline, winner selection method, and claim procedure before activating a real campaign.

How the demonstration works

Stage 1 — Warm-up spin: The first spin is a practice/warm-up round and is not random. It always lands on Try Again and cannot give a prize, gift-draw entry or coupon. This is disclosed on the main page before spinning.

Stage 2 — Gift-draw spin: After the warm-up, visitors may unlock one free demo gift-draw spin directly without any registration. The previously approved Adsterra Native Banner is temporarily paused. Only the site-specific Adsterra Social Bar script remains enabled. Popunder and Native Banner are disabled. Social Bar may display floating sponsored content, subject to visitor opt-out, browser privacy controls, and ad availability. It does not unlock the gift spin, and no rewarded-video ad or verified ad-completion integration is connected. Where privacy law or ad-provider rules require prior consent, that requirement must be implemented and followed. The gift-draw spin randomly chooses one of five sample entries with an equal 20% chance each: Flipkart 50% offer draw entry, Amazon 50% offer draw entry, Meesho 50% offer draw entry, ₹500 draw entry or ₹1,000 draw entry. Each player sees only one gift-draw entry in the demo; some players may get the same result.

No genuine prizes: The five displayed options are draw-entry illustrations, not redeemable coupons, funded prizes or direct wins. Named retailers are not confirmed sponsors or partners. Any actual 50% discount must have verified terms, eligible products, price limits, expiry, sponsor authorization and redemption conditions. The organizer must confirm real prizes and drawing rules before a promotion can be activated.

20-contact sharing (demo)

Visitors can open the Spin & Win link and play directly, without signing up or entering a name, phone number, or email address. After the sample gift spin, they may choose to share the link with 20 interested contacts. This is a suggested demonstration task, not a verified eligibility threshold or a promise of a reward. WhatsApp link openings, clicks, and shares do not prove delivery or 20 different participants. Organizer-only tracking remains private, and no public participant dashboard is required to play. No real prizes are active; do not send unsolicited bulk invitations.

WhatsApp sharing tasks (preview)

After a sample gift entry, the screen shows two sharing options: (1) send the link to 20 different interested contacts, and (2) share it in 5 relevant WhatsApp groups where invitations are allowed. No registration is required to spin or use either share button. The website cannot access WhatsApp contacts/groups or confirm that any invitation was sent. The demo displays the goals of 20 contacts and 5 WhatsApp groups, but it intentionally displays no completed-share counters. The website cannot detect the WhatsApp Send action, selected recipient/group, or actual message delivery. To avoid false completion, opening the WhatsApp share interface, choosing an app, or dismissing a share sheet never marks either task complete. These suggested tasks do not establish eligibility for a real prize. Do not bulk-message strangers or post in groups against their rules. A real promotion would need independently auditable, privacy-respecting rules before any prize eligibility claims.

Registration-free activity measurement

If the backend has been connected, the demonstration can count distinct browser tokens that opened a participant-specific invitation and actually began a spin; the same browser token is counted only once and is not proof of a distinct human. The public visitor can see the count toward a suggested goal of 20; it does not establish that 20 WhatsApp messages were sent or that 20 unique people received them. The public website does not require screenshot uploads and no longer presents the screenshot-proof submission task. Any previously provided screenshots are retained privately for organizer review or deletion. No method on this website verifies actual delivery to five WhatsApp groups or creates prize eligibility. Registration, email, phone number and any payment remain unnecessary. If tracking is offline, no visitor count is saved.

Deepavali results

Target announcement: November 8, 2026, at 8:00 PM Indian Standard Time, subject to organizer confirmation. No winners are preselected, and no results will be invented. Eligibility does not guarantee a prize. Winners must be selected by a documented draw and published only after review.

Security and fairness

No purchase or payment, bank login, OTP, or identity document is required to play this demo. Do not provide any such information to anyone claiming a prize. An actual promotion is subject to applicable local law, platform referral rules, advertising network rules, and published official terms. Incentivized ad views must only be used with an ad provider that expressly permits them.

THE RULES

Giveaway Terms

IMPORTANT: Preview only. This website is an interactive demonstration and no real prize is currently offered. An organizer must publish their legal identity, contact address, verified prize inventory, eligibility criteria, closing deadline, winner selection method, and claim procedure before activating a real campaign.

How the demonstration works

This preview has six equal wheel sectors. Both the first spin and any optional retry are random. The six possible outcomes are Try Again, Flipkart 50% offer draw entry, Amazon 50% offer draw entry, Meesho 50% offer draw entry, ₹500 draw entry, and ₹1,000 draw entry. Each sector has a 1-in-6 chance on each spin. A draw entry ends the round immediately. Only if the first spin lands on Try Again may a visitor unlock one free demo retry; the retry can also land on Try Again. These are draw entries only, not direct wins, real coupons, or funded prizes. The named retailers are not confirmed sponsors or partners. A real 50% discount would need specific eligible products, price limits, expiry dates, sponsor authorization and redemption terms. Maximum one initial spin plus one conditional retry per demo round. No real sponsored advertisement is connected; the demonstration unlocks the retry for free.

Referral qualification

A participant who gets a gift draw entry needs 20 distinct, valid participating referrals to qualify for organizer review. Simply sharing a link with 20 contacts or generating clicks is not proof of unique persons. Browser-only demo referral counts are not recorded between devices. Production tracking requires server-side records and fraud review; participant identifiers are not proof of unique humans. Using fake accounts, bots, or self-referrals is prohibited.

WhatsApp sharing tasks (preview)

After a sample gift entry, the screen shows two separate tasks: (1) share the invitation with 20 different interested people, and (2) share it in 5 relevant WhatsApp groups where such invitations are allowed. In a real campaign, 20 distinct members would need to actually join and be server-verified; sending 20 messages alone does not satisfy the referral requirement. WhatsApp does not provide this website a way to verify posts to five different groups. The demo permits a participant to self-report group shares, but these browser-only checkmarks are not verified and do not confer prize eligibility. Do not bulk-message strangers or post in groups against their rules. A genuine promotion would require an independently verifiable, fair and privacy-respecting group-share policy before launch.

Deepavali results

Target announcement: November 8, 2026, at 8:00 PM Indian Standard Time, subject to organizer confirmation. No winners are preselected, and no results will be invented. Eligibility does not guarantee a prize. Winners must be selected by a documented draw and published only after review.

Security and fairness

No purchase or payment, bank login, OTP, or identity document is required to play this demo. Do not provide any such information to anyone claiming a prize. An actual promotion is subject to applicable local law, platform referral rules, advertising network rules, and published official terms. Incentivized ad views must only be used with an ad provider that expressly permits them.

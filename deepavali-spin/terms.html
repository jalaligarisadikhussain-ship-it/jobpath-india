THE RULES

Giveaway Terms

IMPORTANT: Preview only. This website is an interactive demonstration and no real prize is currently offered. An organizer must publish their legal identity, contact address, verified prize inventory, eligibility criteria, closing deadline, winner selection method, and claim procedure before activating a real campaign.

How the demonstration works

Stage 1 — Warm-up spin: The first spin is a practice/warm-up round and is not random. It always lands on Try Again and cannot give a prize, gift-draw entry or coupon. This is disclosed on the main page before spinning.

Stage 2 — Gift-draw spin: After the warm-up, visitors may unlock one free demo gift-draw spin. The previously approved Adsterra Native Banner is temporarily paused. Two site-specific Adsterra scripts, Social Bar and Popunder, are enabled while the Native Banner remains disabled. Social Bar can show floating ads; Popunder can open a separate ad page during normal browsing interaction. Both are subject to visitor opt-out, browser privacy controls, and ad availability. It does not unlock the gift spin, and no rewarded-video ad or verified ad-completion integration is connected. Where privacy law or ad-provider rules require prior consent, that requirement must be implemented and followed. The gift-draw spin randomly chooses one of five sample entries with an equal 20% chance each: Flipkart 50% offer draw entry, Amazon 50% offer draw entry, Meesho 50% offer draw entry, ₹500 draw entry or ₹1,000 draw entry. Each player sees only one gift-draw entry in the demo; some players may get the same result.

No genuine prizes: The five displayed options are draw-entry illustrations, not redeemable coupons, funded prizes or direct wins. Named retailers are not confirmed sponsors or partners. Any actual 50% discount must have verified terms, eligible products, price limits, expiry, sponsor authorization and redemption conditions. The organizer must confirm real prizes and drawing rules before a promotion can be activated.

Referral qualification

A participant who gets a gift draw entry needs 20 distinct, valid participating referrals to qualify for organizer review. Simply sharing a link with 20 contacts or generating clicks is not proof of unique persons. Browser-only demo referral counts are not recorded between devices. Production tracking requires server-side records and fraud review; participant identifiers are not proof of unique humans. Using fake accounts, bots, or self-referrals is prohibited.

WhatsApp sharing tasks (preview)

After a sample gift entry, the screen shows two separate tasks: (1) share the invitation with 20 different interested people, and (2) share it in 5 relevant WhatsApp groups where such invitations are allowed. In a real campaign, 20 distinct members would need to actually join and be server-verified; sending 20 messages alone does not satisfy the referral requirement. WhatsApp does not provide this website a way to verify posts to five different groups. The demo no longer offers a button that marks group sharing completed. Opening the WhatsApp share interface is not evidence of posting in a group, and verified group progress remains unavailable until an independently auditable verification service exists. Do not bulk-message strangers or post in groups against their rules. A genuine promotion would require an independently verifiable, fair and privacy-respecting group-share policy before launch.

Deepavali results

Target announcement: November 8, 2026, at 8:00 PM Indian Standard Time, subject to organizer confirmation. No winners are preselected, and no results will be invented. Eligibility does not guarantee a prize. Winners must be selected by a documented draw and published only after review.

Security and fairness

No purchase or payment, bank login, OTP, or identity document is required to play this demo. Do not provide any such information to anyone claiming a prize. An actual promotion is subject to applicable local law, platform referral rules, advertising network rules, and published official terms. Incentivized ad views must only be used with an ad provider that expressly permits them.
